# POS Cursor Cooperative (SCSCC)
## Comprehensive Operation Manual & Business Rules

This document serves as the **single source of truth** for the cooperative's business rules, policies, computations, operational procedures, and member benefits. It consolidates the latest updates in business rules with the standard operational guidelines.

---

## 1. MEMBERSHIP & GOVERNANCE

### 1.1 Pre-Membership Education Seminar (PMES)
* Before anybody can be accepted for membership, they are required to complete the prescribed PMES.
* The PMES participant is required to fill out a Participant’s Profile.

### 1.2 Regular Membership
* The cooperative accepts applications for regular members.
* After completing the PMES, the applicant shall fill up a membership form and pay a non-refundable **Membership Fee of ₱100.00**.
* The Education and Training Committee shall recommend, and the Board of Directors (BOD) shall approve membership applications.
* **Minimum Initial Investment**: Members shall pay a minimum paid-up capital of ₱500.00 (equivalent to 5 shares) and subscribe to at least 20 shares (equivalent to ₱2,000.00).

### 1.3 Meetings and General Assembly
* **Ownership Meetings**: Conducted at least once a year (June or July) to update members on financial status, activities, issues, and concerns.
* **Annual General Assembly (AGA)**: Held every second Saturday of March. It is the duty of all members to attend for electing officers, adopting reports, approving plans/budgets, and amending by-laws.
* **Special General Assembly**: Conducted as needed for urgent changes to laws, policies, or other important matters.
* **Officers Meeting**: Conducted at least twice a year for reporting and updating on coop operations.
* **Absentee Rule & Penalty**: Regular members are expected to attend the AGA. If absent without a valid written reason approved by the BOD, their entire Patronage Refund (PR) for that year is **plowed back (converted) to their CBU/Share Capital** instead of being paid out in cash.

## 2. CAPITAL BUILD-UP (CBU) & SHARE CAPITAL
Each member contributes to the cooperative's equity pool via Share Capital (Capital Build-Up).

### 2.1 Computations & Values
* **Share Value**: ₱100.00 per share.
* **Minimum Shares for Petty Cash Loan**: 10 shares (₱1,000.00 paid-up amount).
* **Minimum Maintaining Balance for Dividends**: Only active regular members with recorded paid-up share capital are included in the annual distribution.

---

## 3. COOPERATIVE SAVINGS PROGRAM
Members can deposit and withdraw savings through the cooperative's savings program.

### 3.1 Savings Policies
* **Minimum Maintaining Balance to Earn Interest**: ₱100.00.
* **Annual Interest Rate**: 4.0% per annum (compounded/calculated quarterly). Monthly cut-off date is the 10th of the month.
* **Calculation Method**:
  1. **Average Daily Balance (ADB)** is calculated for each month.
  2. **Average Quarterly Balance** is the average ADB of the 3 months in that quarter.
  3. If Quarterly AMB ≥ ₱100.00, interest is calculated.
* **Deposits and Withdrawals**:
  * The depositor shall use the prescribed form for deposits and withdrawals, duly accomplished.
  * Deposits are made through the treasurer and issued with a corresponding Official Receipt (OR).
  * Withdrawals shall bear the updated savings deposit balance as verified by the posting clerk and approved by the Manager/Chairman/Vice-chairman.
* **Delinquency Restriction**: Savings/Time Deposit shall not be withdrawn when depositors have past due loans; rather, they shall automatically be held and applied as loan payment.

## 4. LENDING POLICIES & LOAN CALCULATOR
The cooperative provides financial assistance under strict risk-management parameters.

### 4.1 Basic Principles and General Policies
* Quality and Volume shall be considered as a management policy and practice. All loans shall be goal and purpose-oriented.
* All members who apply for a loan shall undergo Credit Investigation (CI) and are subject to BOD approval.
* **One Member, One Loan Policy**: A member is strictly limited to one (1) active loan at a time. A new loan application requires settling the previous outstanding balance.
* **Delinquency Restriction**: Members with delinquent accounts have all lending privileges frozen. Loan amounts can be offset against savings, dividends, and patronage refunds.

### 4.2 Loan Categories & Specific Rules
1. **Petty Cash Loans**:
   * Open to regular members needing immediate cash whose Share Capital has reached 10 shares (₱1,000.00).
   * **Limit**: Maximum of 75% of the member's paid-up share capital (CBU). Additional loan amount allowed through 75% of share capital of co-makers.
   * **Terms**: 0% interest, 2% service charge, payable for 1 month only.
   * **Penalty**: 3% per month of remaining balance when past due.
2. **Livelihood / Micro Finance Loan**:
   * Open to self-employed members.
   * **Limit**: Maximum of ₱5,000.00 (depending on CI).
   * **Terms**: 2% interest per month, 2% service charge, payable for 2 months. Daily mode of payment or depending on income source.
   * **Penalty**: 3% per month of remaining balance when past due.
3. **Salary Loan**:
   * Open to employed members only.
   * **Limit**: Maximum of ₱5,000.00 (depending on CI).
   * **Terms**: 2% interest per month, 2% service charge, payable for 2 months. Mode of payment every payday.
   * **Penalty**: 3% per month of remaining balance when past due.
4. **Emergency Loan**:
   * Open to members for emergency needs (e.g., hospitalization, calamity).
   * **Limit**: Maximum of ₱5,000.00 (depending on CI). Requires collateral costing twice the approved loan amount.
   * **Terms**: 0% interest, 2% service charge, payable for 1 month only.
   * **Penalty**: 3% per month of remaining balance when past due.

### 4.3 Deductions & Charges (Standard Breakdown)
For every loan release, standard administrative and risk deductions are automatically applied to the principal:
* **Association Reserve Fund (ARF)**: 2% of principal.
* **Cooperative Project Fund / CBU (CPF)**: 4% of principal.
* **Membership Fee (MEM)**: ₱100.00 (for new registrants).
* **Loan Handling Fee / Mutual Fund (LHF)**: ₱100.00.
* **Risk Share Fund (RSF)**: ₱60.00.
* **Documentary Stamp Tax (DST)**: Flat ₱45.00 for standard loans, or 0.75% of principal for institutional calculations.
* **Processing Fee**: Defaults to 3% of principal.
* **Notarial Fee**: Defaults to ₱300.00.

### 4.4 Repayment & Skip-Sunday Rule
* First payment is scheduled for the day immediately following the release date.
* The system automatically skips **Sundays** when generating daily or weekly repayment schedules.

## 5. NET INCOME & STATUTORY RESERVES
At the end of each fiscal year, the cooperative's Net Income is split into statutory reserve funds and member distributions as mandated by R.A. 9520 (Philippine Cooperative Code).

### 5.1 Statutory Reserves Allocation
* **General Reserve Fund**: 10% of Net Income. Deposited in the bank as a buffer against operational losses.
* **Cooperative Education and Training Fund (CETF)**: 10% of Net Income. Primarily for mandatory trainings & seminars.
* **Community Development Fund (CDF)**: 3% of Net Income. For community projects and outreach.
* **Optional Fund**: 7% of Net Income. Utilized for land, building, computerization, and promotional activities.
* **Net Surplus for Member Distribution**: 70% of Net Income.

### 5.2 Member Benefits Pool (70% Net Surplus)
* **Interest on Share Capital (ISC)**: 70% of the Net Surplus distribution pool. Distributed proportionally based on paid-up share capital.
* **Patronage Refund (PR)**: 30% of the Net Surplus distribution pool. Allocated using a simple equal-share method or proportional to interest paid. (Note: Ploughed back to CBU if member is absent at AGA without valid reason).

## 6. FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT & ACCOUNTING

### 6.1 Standard Double-Entry Account Coding (CDA Aligned)
All transaction postings are automatically routed to the cooperative's General Ledger:
* `1010` - Cash on Hand
* `1020` - Cash in Bank
* `1100` - Loans Receivable
* `2010` - Savings Deposits Payable
* `3010` - Share Capital
* `4010` - Interest on Loans (Income)
* `4020` - Service Charges
* `4030` - Membership Fees
* `4040` - Penalties & Fines
* `4060` - Other Income
* `5xxx` - Operating Expenses

### 6.2 Asset & Liability Management
* **Properties & Equipment**: Any purchase, improvement, or repair must be approved by the BOD. The safekeeping of properties is the responsibility of the designated property custodian.
* **Outside Borrowings**: Must be obtained from the least-cost source and comply with agreements. Cannot exceed the loan limit set by BOD resolution.
* **Donations**: May be made for charitable organizations up to a maximum of ₱10,000.00 per year, supported by a BOD Resolution.

### 6.3 Internal Control & Policies
* **Cash Disbursement**: Authorized signatories are the Chairperson and Treasurer (Vice-Chairperson assumes in the absence of the Chairperson). Two signatures are required.
* **Cash Receipts**: Pre-numbered receipts must be used for any cash received. The Bookkeeper prepares quarterly financial statements, and the Treasurer prepares monthly statements.
* **Separation of Duties**: Approving, processing, recording, reviewing, and custody should be separated. The cashier should not post payments to accounts.

---

## 7. HUMAN RESOURCE DEVELOPMENT
* **Hiring & Firing**: Selection is based on competence. Management administers hiring. Gross negligence is a ground for termination, adhering to the Philippine Labor Law.
* **Staff Development**: The BOD Vice-Chairman (ETC Ex-officio chairman) prepares the staff development plan.
* **Compensation & Benefits**: BOD determines employee compensation and honorarium for officers. Employees receive a year-end bonus subject to fund availability.
* **Performance Appraisal**: Formal appraisals for officers and employees are conducted at least annually. Salary adjustments are based on job performance appraisals.
