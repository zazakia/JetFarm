## Check Requested

Verify whether the manual covers this allocation:

- Net Income breakdown:
  - 70% Net Surplus
  - 10% General Reserve Fund
  - 7% Optional Fund
  - 3% Community Development Fund
  - 10% Education and Training Fund
- Sharing in Net Surplus:
  - 70% Interest on Share Capital
  - 30% Patronage Refund

## Findings

The current manual partially covers the requested allocation.

Section 5, "Policies on Statutory Reserves and Interest on Share Capital & Patronage Refund Distribution," currently states:

- General Reserve Fund: 10%
- Optional Fund: 7%
- Community Development Fund: 3%
- CETF / Education and Training Fund: 10%
- Interest on Share Capital and Patronage Refund: mentioned, but no percentage split is stated

The listed statutory funds total 30%. The manual does not explicitly state that the remaining 70% is Net Surplus available for distribution. It also does not state that the 70% Net Surplus is divided 70% to Interest on Share Capital and 30% to Patronage Refund.

## Gaps To Fix

1. Add an explicit "Net Income Allocation" sentence or table showing that 100% of net income is allocated as 70% Net Surplus plus the listed 30% funds.
2. Add an explicit "Sharing in Net Surplus" clause showing:
   - 70% Interest on Share Capital
   - 30% Patronage Refund
3. Standardize wording:
   - Use "General Reserve Fund," not "Reserved Fund," unless the cooperative's approved policy uses that exact term.
   - Use "Share Capital," not "Shared Capital."
   - Use consistent capitalization for "Community Development Fund" and "Education and Training Fund."

## Recommended Replacement Text

Replace the current Section 5 allocation block with:

```text
5. POLICIES ON NET INCOME ALLOCATION, STATUTORY RESERVES, INTEREST ON SHARE CAPITAL, AND PATRONAGE REFUND DISTRIBUTION

The Cooperative's net income shall be allocated as follows:

1. Net Surplus for Distribution to Members - 70%
2. General Reserve Fund - 10%
3. Optional Fund - 7%
4. Community Development Fund - 3%
5. Education and Training Fund / CETF - 10%

The Net Surplus for Distribution to Members shall be distributed as follows:

1. Interest on Share Capital - 70% of the Net Surplus for Distribution to Members
2. Patronage Refund - 30% of the Net Surplus for Distribution to Members

The General Reserve Fund shall be deposited in the bank. The Optional Fund shall be deposited in the bank and may be utilized for land, building, computerization, and promotional activities. The Community Development Fund shall be used for community development projects, solicitations, donations, and community outreach programs. The Education and Training Fund / CETF shall be used primarily for mandatory trainings and seminars for cooperative officers and staff, subscriptions to relevant publications, attendance at cooperative summits, congresses, trainings, workshops, and Coop Month Celebration expenses.

Patronage Refund of a member who does not attend the General Assembly meeting without a valid reason communicated in advance and approved by the Board shall be plowed back to his or her Capital Build-Up (CBU).
```

## Conclusion

The requested formula is mathematically complete:

- Net Income allocation: 70% + 10% + 7% + 3% + 10% = 100%
- Net Surplus sharing: 70% + 30% = 100%

The manual needs an update because it currently documents only the 30% statutory fund side clearly and leaves the 70% distribution and 70/30 member-sharing formula unstated.
