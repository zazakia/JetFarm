Executive Summary

In the rapidly evolving financial landscape of the Philippines, savings and credit cooperatives require robust, reliable, and legally compliant tools to manage assets, streamline field collections, and ensure regulatory transparency. POSCursor (customized for the POS Cursor Cooperative) is an enterprise-grade, mobile-first, and offline-first Loan and Savings Management System (LMS) specifically designed to address these requirements.

POSCursor bridges the gap between field operations and back-office financial accounting. Field collectors operate in remote areas without internet access, recording loan collections and member deposits securely on their mobile devices. Once a connection is established, the application automatically synchronizes data back to a secure cloud-hosted PostgreSQL database managed via Supabase. Behind the scenes, a comprehensive double-entry accounting engine automatically posts entries to a Chart of Accounts aligned with the Cooperative Development Authority (CDA) standards, providing instant compliance reporting, balance sheets, and operations statements.

This proposal outlines the platform's core architecture, detailed role-based features, benefits, and compliance modules (including CDA-format statements, automatic statutory fund allocations, and credit reporting exports for the Credit Information Corporation (CIC)).

Core Value Proposition

  • Offline-First Resilience: field operations continue uninterrupted without internet, ensuring zero data loss during rural collections.
  • Automated Regulatory Compliance: instant CDA financial statements, statutory reserve splits, and automated credit registry reporting files for the CIC.
  • Auditability and Transparency: end-to-end audit trails, immutable collection sheets, cash handover validation (remittances), and role-based permissions prevent internal leakages.
  • Integrated Accounting: direct ledger integration ensures that every transaction (loan release, payment, expense, savings interest) is posted automatically, eliminating manual reconciliation overhead.

1. System Architecture & Tech Stack

The platform utilizes a modern, resilient technology stack to achieve high responsiveness, robust security, offline persistence, and seamless sync capabilities:

Layer Technology Components Purpose & Strategic Benefit
Mobile & Web Frontend React Native, Expo SDK 55, Expo Router, NativeWind 4 Cross-platform native applications for iOS and Android, plus an admin web dashboard. Leverages file-based routing and atomic design for fluid performance.
Local Database WatermelonDB (SQLite local backend) Optimized for high-performance offline data caching, handling millions of records on low-end devices with asynchronous database queries.
Cloud Backend & DB Supabase (PostgreSQL, Supabase Auth, Storage) Relational integrity, real-time database listener, secure JWT authentication, and automatic daily cloud backup storage.
Security & Encryption AES-256 at-rest encryption, Row-Level Security (RLS) PII (Personally Identifiable Information) such as member addresses and phone numbers are encrypted locally and in the cloud database. RLS restricts access so users only see authorized data.
Testing & Verification Jest, Playwright E2E testing Continuous testing of calculations, workflows, and UI transitions to ensure zero regressions before production deployment.

Offline-First Sync Engine (WatermelonDB ↔ Supabase)

Field collectors frequently operate in barangays with spotty or non-existent cellular coverage. To solve this, POSCursor uses a custom synchronization service:

  1. Local Write: Transactions are written instantly to the local SQLite database. The user interface updates immediately, ensuring zero lag.
  2. Status Tracking: The database marks records as created, updated, or deleted locally.
  3. Bidirectional Synchronization: When connectivity is detected, the system pulls remote updates (ensuring field data matches the central office) and pushes local modifications to Supabase, handling conflict resolution through chronological precedence.

2. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

To preserve operational security and adhere to internal control best practices (separation of duties), the system restricts access through six (6) distinct user roles:

Role Operational Scope Key Benefits
Admin Full system configuration, financial settings, audit log review, statutory fund distributions, and final report generation. Complete administrative control, system auditing, and strategic oversight.
Collector Access to assigned routes, collection sheets, recording member payments/deposits (offline), and submitting cash remittances. Simplifies field data collection, eliminates paper sheets, and calculates cash-on-hand.
Loan-Encoder Encodes borrower profiles, performs credit investigations (CI), draft loans, and runs loan calculators. Ensures compliance with credit policies prior to board approval and disbursement.
Payment-Encoder Records walk-in payments at the central office, manages over-the-counter transactions, and issues digital receipts. Decouples cash collection from accounting posting, enforcing internal controls.
Expenses-Encoder Logs operational disbursements, utility costs, salaries, and other daily cash outflows. Centralizes cost tracking and ensures all expenses flow directly into the income statements.
Borrower Self-service portal to view outstanding loan balances, repayment schedules, and personal savings ledgers. Enhances member trust and reduces inquiry load on cooperative staff.

3. Platform Modules & Features: Purpose and Benefits

3.1 Membership & Governance Module

Purpose: To manage the lifetime member journey, from initial Pre-Membership Education Seminar (PMES) training to membership approval and general assembly registry tracking.

  • PMES Participant Tracking: Registers and tracks attendees of the mandatory PMES. Only certified participants can apply for membership.
    Benefit: Strict adherence to Philippine cooperative bylaws on education.
  • Onboarding & Fee Registry: Digitizes membership applications, collects the non-refundable ₱100.00 Membership Fee, and routes applications to the Education & Board of Directors for approval.
    Benefit: Speeds up onboarding and eliminates paper filing backlogs.
  • Annual General Assembly (AGA) Registry & Attendance: Tracks attendance during the annual assembly.
    Benefit: Facilitates quorum validation and automatically implements the AGA Absentee Rule.
  • Absentee Rule Automation: Members absent from the AGA without a valid, BOD-approved written reason have their annual Patronage Refund automatically plowed back and converted directly into their Capital Build-Up (CBU)/Share Capital.
    Benefit: Incentivizes active governance participation while strengthening the cooperative's capital base.

3.2 Capital Build-Up (CBU) & Share Capital Management

Purpose: To track, allocate, and grow the cooperative's core equity contributions (Share Capital) from its members.

  • Subscription Tracking: Tracks each member's subscribed shares (minimum 20 shares or ₱2,000.00) against their actual paid-up capital (minimum ₱500.00 initial investment).
    Benefit: Monitors compliance with equity commitments and tracks outstanding subscription receivables.
  • Share Valuation Engine: Enforces the standard ₱100.00 par value per share.
    Benefit: Prevents calculation errors and guarantees uniform share distribution.
  • Dividend Eligibility Gates: Enforces minimum maintaining balances and active status conditions for annual dividend distributions.
    Benefit: Ensures distributions are allocated strictly to compliant, active members.

3.3 Cooperative Savings Program

Purpose: To provide a secure deposit facility for members while calculating accurate quarterly interest payouts based on member savings behavior.

  • Average Daily Balance (ADB) Calculator: Automatically tracks deposits and withdrawals to calculate each member's monthly ADB.
    Benefit: Replaces complex manual spreadsheets with real-time, error-free interest computations.
  • Quarterly Compounding Engine: Compounds interest quarterly at the cooperative rate of 4.0% per annum, subject to a minimum maintaining balance of ₱100.00.
    Benefit: Incentivizes savings and updates member passbooks automatically.
  • Delinquency Savings Freeze: Automatically holds savings accounts when a member has an outstanding, past-due loan. Withdrawals are blocked, and balances can be automatically applied to outstanding debts.
    Benefit: Protects cooperative assets and reduces non-performing loans (NPLs).

3.4 Advanced Lending Module & Loan Calculator

Purpose: To enforce lending policies, perform credit assessments, compute releases, deduct statutory fees, and schedule collections.

  • Loan Classification System: Pre-configures specific business rules for four loan types:
    1. Petty Cash Loan: Maximum of 75% of paid-up capital, 0% interest, 2% service charge, 1-month term.
    2. Livelihood/Micro Finance Loan: Up to ₱5,000.00, 2% interest/month, 2% service charge, 2-month term.
    3. Salary Loan: Up to ₱5,000.00, 2% interest/month, 2% service charge, 2-month term.
    4. Emergency Loan: Up to ₱5,000.00 (requires collateral), 0% interest, 2% service charge, 1-month term.
    Benefit: Hardcodes policy limits directly into the software, preventing unauthorized overrides.
  • Credit Investigation (CI) Log: Records credit investigation details, co-maker guarantees, and collateral valuations prior to board approval.
    Benefit: Creates an audit trail of credit assessments, protecting capital.
  • Automated Loan Deductions: Automatically computes and deducts standard charges from the gross loan principal upon release:
    • Association Reserve Fund (ARF): 2% of principal.
    • Cooperative Project Fund / CBU (CPF): 4% of principal.
    • Processing Fee: 3% of principal.
    • Documentary Stamp Tax (DST): Flat ₱45.00 (standard) or 0.75% of principal (institutional).
    • Notarial Fee (₱300.00), Risk Share Fund (₱60.00), and Loan Handling Fee (₱100.00).
    Benefit: Guarantees exact net release calculations and automatically posts fees to the appropriate revenue accounts.
  • Repayment Schedule Generator: Automatically creates daily, weekly, or semi-monthly amortization schedules based on the release date. Includes the Skip-Sunday Rule to ensure no collections are scheduled on Sundays.
    Benefit: Standardizes expectations and optimizes the collector's daily routing schedule.

3.5 Field Collection & Remittance Workflow

Purpose: To secure the daily collection pipeline, validate field cash collections, and track collector efficiency metrics.

  • Offline Digital Collection Sheet: Lists borrowers by route and indicates their expected daily amortization. Collector inputs payment amounts offline.
    Benefit: Eliminates paper sheets, reduces input errors, and functions in remote areas.
  • GPS Location Mapping: Logs the geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude) of every collection transaction.
    Benefit: Verifies field collector activity and provides location verification for audits.
  • Remittance Approval Workflow: Field collections calculate a "Cash on Hand" total for the collector. The collector hands over cash to the treasurer, who reviews the collection log and clicks "Approve" or "Reject".
    Benefit: Establishes strict custody transfers, preventing cash leakages.

4. CDA Compliance & Accounting Integration

POSCursor is built around a double-entry general ledger accounting system. Rather than tracking balances in isolation, every operational action triggers a structured accounting entry that updates the general ledger.

4.1 CDA-Aligned Chart of Accounts

The system pre-configures standard account codes set by the Cooperative Development Authority (CDA) of the Philippines:

Account Code Account Title Automated Operations
1010 Cash on Hand Debited upon collection sheet approval; Credited upon loan release.
1020 Cash in Bank Updated upon bank deposit or payroll bank disbursements.
1100 Loans Receivable Debited upon gross loan release; Credited when principal payments are posted.
2010 Savings Deposits Payable Credited during member deposits; Debited during withdrawals or past-due loan offsets.
3010 Share Capital (CBU) Credited upon share purchases or plowed-back patronage refunds.
4010 Interest Income on Loans Credited automatically when loan interest is collected.
4020 Service Charges Credited automatically during loan deduction calculations.
5000+ Operating Expenses Debited when expenses are encoded by the Expenses-Encoder.

4.2 Accounting Period Controls

  • Accounting Period History: Tracks active and historical periods.
    Benefit: Organizes financial data chronologically.
  • Monthly & Yearly Closing Locks: Admins can officially close an accounting period. Once closed, no historical transactions can be added, modified, or deleted.
    Benefit: Enforces audit integrity, prevents retrospective adjustments, and ensures financial reports match submitted tax and regulatory records.

5. Compliance & Automated Reporting Modules

Regulatory reporting is often a bottleneck for cooperatives, requiring weeks of manual data assembly. POSCursor automates these reports, generating them directly from the ledger.

5.1 CDA-Aligned Financial Reports

The system outputs reports formatted to match the Cooperative Development Authority standards:

  • Statement of Financial Condition (Balance Sheet): Lists Assets, Liabilities, and Equity (including Statutory Funds and Retained Surplus).
    Benefit: Shows the cooperative's real-time financial health.
  • Statement of Operations (Income Statement): Details credit revenues (interest, service charges, penalties) and operational expenses.
    Benefit: Computes Net Surplus (income) for any given period.
  • Statement of Changes in Equity: Tracks subscriptions, capital build-ups, and statutory reserve additions.
    Benefit: Documents capital growth.
  • Notes to Financial Statements: Generates standard disclosure notes and breakdown ledgers.
    Benefit: Speeds up annual audit preparation.

5.2 Automated Statutory Reserve Allocations

Under R.A. 9520 (Philippine Cooperative Code), net surplus must be divided into statutory reserve funds. POSCursor automates these splits at year-end:

Fund Percentage Regulatory Purpose & System Action
General Reserve Fund 10.0% Buffer for operational losses. Locked in account code 3020; cannot be distributed.
Education & Training Fund (CETF) 10.0% Allocated to account code 3030. Splits 50% for local coop trainings and 50% remitted to the union/federation.
Community Development Fund (CDF) 3.0% Allocated to account code 3040 for community outreach projects.
Optional Fund 7.0% Allocated to account code 3050 for land, buildings, and computerization projects.
Distributable Net Surplus 70.0% Transferred to the member benefits pool. Splits into 70% Interest on Share Capital (ISC) and 30% Patronage Refund (PR).

5.3 CIC Automated Credit Reporting Module

Regulatory Context: Republic Act No. 9510 (Credit Information System Act) mandates that all lending institutions and cooperatives in the Philippines submit basic credit data and update member credit records monthly with the Credit Information Corporation (CIC).

System Features & Functions:

  • Data Formatting: Extracts borrower profiles, loan balances, repayment histories, and delinquency statuses, formatting them to meet CIC requirements.
  • Automated Extraction: Consolidates data from the database, eliminating manual data entry.
  • Validation Checks: Validates fields (e.g., tax numbers, birthdates) before output to prevent transmission errors.
  • Submission Ready: Outputs a standardized submission file for direct upload to the CIC portal.

Benefits:

  • Ensures full regulatory compliance, avoiding penalties from the CIC.
  • Saves staff time by automating data compilation.
  • Promotes credit transparency and helps members build verifiable credit histories.

5.4 LandBank Payroll & Disbursement Register Export

Purpose: To automate loan releases and dividend distributions by generating payroll register files compatible with Land Bank of the Philippines (LBP) banking portals.

  • Account Mapping: Links member records to verified LBP account numbers.
  • Automatic Payroll Generation: Groups approved disbursements and formats them to meet LandBank requirements.
  • Register File Export: Outputs a text or CSV file containing account names, numbers, and disbursement amounts.
  • Benefits:
    Saves Time: Eliminates the need to enter disbursements manually into banking portals.
    Reduces Risk: Prevents typos, ensuring funds are sent to the correct accounts.

5.5 Creditors Group Life (CGL) Insurance Summary

Purpose: To manage group life insurance coverage for active borrowers, protecting the cooperative from outstanding debt loss if a borrower passes away.

  • Insurance List: Lists active borrowers, loan amounts, and coverage dates.
  • Automatic Premium Calculator: Computes premium deductions based on loan amounts and terms.
  • Reporting: Generates monthly summaries for submission to the insurance provider.
  • Benefits:
    Mitigates Risk: Ensures all active loans are covered by insurance.
    Simplifies Admin Work: Automates the creation of monthly insurance registers.
