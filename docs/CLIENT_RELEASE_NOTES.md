# POSCURSOR - COOP: Summary of System Updates & Enhancements
*(Client & Stakeholder Version — From Newest to Oldest)*

---

### Executive Overview

This summary provides a simple, easy-to-read overview of all new features, improvements, and system updates completed for **POSCURSOR - COOP** from **August 2026 down to June 2026**. 

All updates have been designed to make field operations smoother for collectors, simplify financial accounting for admin staff, ensure member deposit accuracy, and comply with Cooperative Development Authority (CDA) standards.

---

### 1. August 5, 2026 — Financial Automation, Bank Transfers & System Polish

- 🏦 **Bank Account & Cash Vault Transfers**:
  - Management can now record direct fund transfers between bank accounts and the central cash box vault seamlessly, with full tracking of deposits, withdrawals, and bank ledgers.
- ⚡ **Automated Bookkeeping & Journal Posting**:
  - Everyday transactions—including loan releases, payment collections, expense payments, and member savings deposits—are now automatically posted into the general accounting ledger, eliminating manual entry mistakes.
- 🔍 **Loan Data & Account Verification**:
  - Standardized all chart-of-account codes and verified historical loan schedules against master spreadsheets (including Abrenillo & Gonzales accounts) to ensure 100% accurate balance totals.
- 👁️ **Easier Reading & Visual Improvements**:
  - Improved screen text, color contrast, and buttons so staff and collectors can easily read details in any lighting environment. Added a printable application form preview for loan requests.

---

### 2. August 1, 2026 — Enhanced Member Profiles & Savings Breakdown

- 👤 **Complete Member Financial View**:
  - Admin staff can now view complete transaction histories for every member’s Savings and Share Capital (CBU) accounts directly on their profile page.
- 🚀 **Faster Member Account Loading**:
  - Optimized system balance lookups so member account histories load instantly when opened.

---

### 3. July 31, 2026 — Field Collector Commissions & Expense Management

- 💼 **Automated Collector Commission Calculator**:
  - Added an automatic commission calculator for field collectors based on their daily collection volume, assigned areas, and performance goals.
- 📝 **Flexible & Safe Expense Management**:
  - Allowed admin users to edit logged expense entries while protecting essential business expense categories (like Petty Cash) from accidental removal.

---

### 4. July 30, 2026 — Share Capital Reports & Member Dividend Engine

- 📊 **Member Equity & Share Capital Reports**:
  - Created clear report views showing individual member share balances, average monthly deposits, and total equity accumulated over time.
- ⚖️ **CDA-Compliant Dividend Calculations**:
  - Automated the calculation of Patronage Dividends and Interest on Share Capital in strict compliance with CDA regulatory rules.
- 🔒 **Data Protection & Secure Backup**:
  - Reinforced cloud backup rules to guarantee that all financial data and member payments remain safe and continuously synced.

---

### 5. July 29, 2026 — Official POSCURSOR Rebrand & Online Web Access

- 🌟 **New POSCURSOR Cooperative Identity**:
  - Officially updated the mobile app and web portal logo, title, color scheme, and login screens to reflect the **POSCURSOR - COOP** brand.
- 💵 **Cash-on-Hand & Miscellaneous Income**:
  - Enhanced the Cash-on-Hand screen to easily record extra income sources, bank withdrawals, and manual petty cash top-ups.
- 🌐 **Cloud Web Portal Launch (`jetfarm.pages.dev`)**:
  - Published a dedicated web portal version for office staff to access dashboards, reports, and administrative controls from any desktop computer or laptop.

---

### 6. July 28, 2026 — Cooperative Business Modules & Member Marketplace

- 🏪 **Multi-Purpose Business Modules (MPCO)**:
  - Prepared the platform to support additional cooperative business lines (such as Agri-store, Pharmacy, Transport, and Consumer Store).
- 🛍️ **Digital Member Marketplace & Seller Center**:
  - Introduced a marketplace feature allowing cooperative members to list items, manage inventory, receive orders, and track payouts inside the app.

---

### 7. July 23, 2026 — Savings Account Ledgers & In-App Release Log

- 💳 **Separate Account Ledgers**:
  - Separated general member registration records from individual savings and share capital account ledgers, guaranteeing deposit safety.
- 📱 **In-App "App Updates" Screen**:
  - Added a dedicated "App Updates" screen in the app menu so staff and members can view recently released features and fixes anytime.

---

### 8. July 14, 2026 — Cash Box Vault Connection & Fair Loan Breakdown

- 🔐 **Real-Time Cash Box Vault Connection**:
  - Connected the central Cash Box Vault balance to all active transactions—automatically updating vault cash whenever payments are received, loans are disbursed, or savings are deposited.
- 📄 **Fair & Transparent Loan Deductions**:
  - Corrected upfront loan deduction processing (handling DST, ROD, and LTO fees properly) so loan computation slips match exact release amounts.
- 📖 **Member Operations Manual & Auto-Sync**:
  - Added an interactive Member Operation Manual into the borrower menu and enabled automatic data sync whenever the app is opened.

---

### 9. July 5–13, 2026 — Dynamic Settings & Official CDA Financial Reports

- ⚙️ **Flexible Admin Settings**:
  - Moved interest rates, minimum required balances, and reserve allocation percentages into an easy Admin Settings screen, allowing management to adjust rates as policies evolve.
- 📈 **Official CDA Financial Statements**:
  - Added official Balance Sheet, Income Statement, Statement of Changes in Equity, and Trial Balance reports tailored to CDA standard formats.
- 📅 **Multi-Year Historical Import**:
  - Imported audited historical data for Fiscal Years 2024 and 2025 so management reports can show multi-year comparative trends.

---

### 10. June 29 – July 2, 2026 — Member Patronage Dividends & Field Offline Sync

- 🎁 **Patronage Dividend Distribution**:
  - Created a distribution tool to compute and award member dividend payouts based on their active loan interest payments and share contributions.
- 📶 **Reliable Offline-First Sync**:
  - Enabled field collectors to collect payments in remote areas without internet. All collections are saved locally on the phone and automatically upload to the cloud server once internet connection is restored.
