## 2. MEMBERSHIP

### 2.1 Pre-Membership Education Seminar (PMES)
- **Mandatory** before membership acceptance
- Participants complete a "Participant's Profile" form
- Education & Training Committee (ETC) conducts the seminar

### 2.2 Membership Types
| Type | Requirements | Privileges |
|------|-------------|------------|
| **Associate** | Complete PMES, pay ₱200 fee | Limited — no voting rights, cannot borrow |
| **Regular** | Associate + min ₱500 paid-up capital (5 shares) + subscribe to 20 shares (₱2,000) | Full — can vote, borrow, attend GA |

### 2.3 Membership Application Workflow
1. Complete PMES → receive certificate
2. Fill membership application form
3. Pay **non-refundable membership fee: ₱200.00**
4. Education & Training Committee **recommends**
5. Board of Directors **approves** (issues BOD Resolution)
6. Subscribe to minimum 20 shares (₱2,000.00)
7. Pay minimum paid-up capital of ₱500.00 (5 shares)
8. Receive Membership Number

### 2.4 Member Profile Data (from Registry of Membership form)
- Membership Number, TIN Number
- Full Name (Surname, First Name, Middle Name)
- Date Accepted, BOD Resolution Number
- Type/Kind of Membership (Associate / Regular)
- Initial Capital Subscription: Number of Shares, Amount Subscribed, Initial Paid-up
- Place of Birth, Date of Birth, Age, Sex, Civil Status
- Residential Address: Street & No., Barangay, City/Municipality, Province, Zip Code
- Religion/Social Affiliation
- Contact: Residence Tel, Office Tel, Mobile No, Email
- Highest Educational Attainment (Elementary → Vocational)
- Occupation / Major Source of Income
- Annual Income (in PHP)
- Spouse Information (if legally married): Name, DOB, Age, Occupation, Tel/Cellphone
- Children: Full Name, DOB, Age (up to 7)
- Termination: BOD Resolution Number, Date of Termination

### 2.5 Membership Termination
- Requires BOD Resolution
- Grounds: Fine, Suspension, or Expulsion
- Share capital is settled per cooperative bylaws
- Recorded in Registry of Membership

### 3.2 Rules
- Members subscribe to shares upon membership
- Can pay in installments per chosen schedule
- Share capital earns annual **Interest on Share Capital** (rate set by BOD)
- Share capital is the basis for **Petty Cash Loan** eligibility (75% loanable)
- Members must contribute at least a % of annual share capital interest + patronage refund to their share capital
- Failure to comply → fine of ₱___ per month on default amount

### 3.3 Capital Build-Up (CBU)
- Patronage refund for members NOT attending GA (without valid pre-approved excuse) is **plowed back to CBU**
- CBU increases the member's total share capital

## 4. LOAN PRODUCTS

### 4.1 General Lending Principles
1. Quality and volume are management policy benchmarks
2. All loans must be **goal/purpose-oriented**
3. All applicants undergo **Credit Investigation (CI)** and require **BOD approval**
4. Loans secured against assigned: savings deposits, share capital interest/dividends, patronage refund, collectibles/benefits
5. **Auto-freeze on delinquency**: savings, share capital interest, dividends, patronage refund, collectibles automatically frozen and applied to delinquent installments
6. **Renewal only after full payment** of previous loan
7. **Credit Committee** evaluates and approves all applications

### 4.2 Loan Types

#### Petty Cash Loan
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Eligibility | Regular members with ≥10 shares (₱1,000+ share capital) |
| Maximum Amount | 75% of share capital |
| Additional Amount | +75% of co-maker's share capital |
| Interest Rate | 0% |
| Service Charge | 2% |
| Term | 1 month |
| Penalty | 3% per month on remaining balance past due |
| Renewal | Upon full payment |

#### Livelihood / Microfinance Loan
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Eligibility | Self-employed members |
| Maximum Amount | ₱5,000 (CI-dependent) |
| Interest Rate | 2% per month |
| Service Charge | 2% |
| Term | 2 months |
| Penalty | 3% per month on remaining balance past due |
| Payment Mode | Daily or per income source |

#### Salary Loan
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Eligibility | Employed members only |
| Maximum Amount | ₱5,000 (CI-dependent) |
| Interest Rate | 2% per month |
| Service Charge | 2% |
| Term | 2 months |

#### Emergency Loan
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Eligibility | Members with urgent needs (hospitalization, calamity) |
| Maximum Amount | ₱5,000 (CI-dependent) |
| Collateral | Required — worth 2× approved loan amount |
| Interest Rate | 0% |
| Service Charge | 2% |
| Term | 1 month |
| Penalty | 3% per month on remaining balance past due |

### 4.3 Penalty Calculation
```
penalty = remaining_balance × 0.03 × months_past_due
```

### 4.4 Delinquency Auto-Deduction
When a member becomes delinquent:
1. Savings deposit → **frozen**, unavailable for withdrawal
2. Interest on Share Capital → **frozen**
3. Dividends → **frozen**
4. Patronage Refund → **frozen**
5. Collectibles/Benefits → **frozen**
6. All frozen amounts **automatically applied** to installments due

## 5. SAVINGS / TIME DEPOSIT

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Minimum Maintaining Balance | ₱100.00 |
| Interest Rate | 4% per annum |
| Interest Computation | Quarterly, on average monthly balance |
| Monthly Cut-off | 10th of the month |
| Forms | Prescribed deposit/withdrawal forms |
| Deposit Processing | Through Treasurer → Official Receipt (OR) issued |
| Withdrawal Approval | Treasurer → Posting Clerk verification → Manager/Chairman/Vice-Chairman approval |
| Delinquent Members | **Cannot withdraw** — savings applied to loan payment |

### Interest Calculation
```
quarterly_interest = (average_monthly_balance_for_quarter) × (0.04 / 4)
average_monthly_balance = sum of daily balances / days in month
```
Cut-off: 10th of each month defines the monthly period.

## 7. FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT

### 7.1 Surplus Allocation (CDA-Mandated)
| Fund | Percentage | Purpose |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Net Surplus for Distribution to Members | 70% of net income | Split 70% Interest on Share Capital / 30% Patronage Refund |
| General Reserve Fund | 10% of net income | 100% deposited to bank |
| Optional Fund | 7% of net income | Land, building, computerization, promotions |
| Community Development Fund | 3% of net income | Community projects, donations, outreach |
| CETF (Education & Training) | 10% of net income | 50% mandatory trainings/seminars, 50% other (publications, summits, Coop Month) |
| Interest on Share Capital | 70% of net surplus for distribution | Annual dividend on member share capital |
| Patronage Refund | 30% of net surplus for distribution | Distributed to members; non-GA attendees' refund → CBU |

### 7.2 Donation Policy
- For charitable organizations and community goodwill
- Requires BOD Resolution with necessity and amount
- Form: check or in-kind, in beneficiary's name
- Beneficiary must acknowledge with documentation
- **Annual cap: ₱10,000.00**

### 7.3 Borrowing Policy
- Major borrowings from least-cost source
- Must comply with existing loan agreements
- Cannot exceed Board-set loan limit
- Board + Manager review limit annually

| Statement of Changes in Equity | Quarterly | Bookkeeper |

### 8.3 Internal Control Principles
1. **Segregation of duties**: Approving, processing, recording, reviewing, and custody must be separate
2. **Cashier restriction**: May NOT post payments to accounts
3. **Dual signature**: Chairperson + Treasurer co-sign all checks
4. **Pre-numbered forms**: Receipts, checks, critical documents
5. **Physical controls**: Locked safes, cabinets, controlled blank checks
6. **Digital security**: Password-protected computer data
7. **Authorization chain**: Clear chain of command, authorized signatures required
8. **Periodic inspection**: All transactions periodically reviewed with approved corrections
